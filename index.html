How it plays
Noms, as the crew plays it.

Sixteen rounds. One card up to seven, then No Trumps (7 cards), then Blind (7 cards), then back down seven to one.

Trumps. After the deal the next card is turned up — that suit is trumps. No turn-up in the No Trumps round.

Noms. Starting left of the dealer, everyone nominates how many tricks they'll take. The dealer noms last and can't take the total to the number of cards dealt — someone always misses.

Blind round. Nom before you look at your hand.

Play. Left of dealer leads. Follow suit if you can. Highest trump wins the trick, otherwise highest card of the suit led. Winner leads next.

Scoring. +1 a trick, +10 for landing your nom exactly.

The money. £2 each in the pot — 1st and 2nd take half each. The winner also collects 5p a point off every other player, rounded up to the nearest 50p per player.
